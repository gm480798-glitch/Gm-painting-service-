Turning a handed-over flat into your own

Hennur, along Hennur Main Road, is developing quickly, with many newer apartment and gated projects. New owners often find that handed-over walls have patches, marks from other trades and an uneven builder finish that they would rather fix before moving in.

GM Painting Service helps with exactly that. What homes in Hennur usually need painted

Builder-finish walls with patches

Patchy paint and marks from electricians, plumbers and carpenters are common at handover. We repair and recoat so the whole flat looks uniform, not touched up in spots.

Painting before furniture arrives

An empty flat is the ideal time to paint. We finish before furniture and appliances come in, so nothing needs covering or moving and drying time is not a problem.

Full repaint or targeted touch-up?

Some new flats need a complete repaint, others only touch-ups on high-visibility walls. We look at the finish and tell you honestly which makes sense for your budget.

Hennur painting FAQs

Does a new flat really need a full repaint?

Not always. We inspect the walls and advise between touch-ups and complete painting, so you spend only where it makes a visible difference.

Can you finish painting before I move into my Hennur flat?

Yes, and we recommend it. An empty flat is faster to paint and avoids covering furniture; share the possession date and we plan around it.

Do you apply putty on new walls?

Where it improves the finish, yes. The putty and primer details are stated in the written quotation so there are no surprises.

Can I add a feature wall during the same job?

Yes. We can add a texture or designer wall to the schedule and quote it after viewing the wall.
