Common painting requests in Ejipura

Compact rental flats between tenants

Small flats are quick to turn around when the job is planned. We repair scuffs and holes, paint in a neutral shade and clean up so the flat can be shown quickly.

Ground-floor walls with damp marks

Lower walls of older buildings can show damp marks near the base. We check the source before painting so the stain does not come through again.

Owners watching the budget

Not every wall needs the top paint. We help you choose where to spend, such as a premium finish in the living room and economical coats elsewhere.
