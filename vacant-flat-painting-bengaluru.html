Why an empty flat is the best time to repaint

When a flat is vacant, our painters can sand, patch, prime and recoat without working around beds, sofas and wardrobes. Every wall, ceiling corner and skirting line is reachable, which helps us deliver excellent finishing and a smooth, even surface. It is also the most affordable moment to repaint, because the work is simpler and our unfurnished packages are priced lower than furnished ones.

What we check in an empty flat

  • Damp patches, peeling paint and hairline cracks, which are fixed or flagged before the new coat goes on.
  • Old nail holes, dents and rough patches, filled and sanded level.
  • Ceiling stains: we look for the source first, because paint cannot stop a leak.
  • Bathroom, kitchen and balcony walls, which need the right preparation.
  • Switchboards, fittings and floors, masked and protected before painting.

Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to paint a vacant flat?

Our unfurnished packages are priced lower than the furnished ones because there is no furniture to move and cover. The exact figures are in the table above, and the final price is confirmed after inspection.

Do I need to be present while the flat is painted?

Not always. Someone should be available for the site check and to approve the quotation. After that, access can be arranged with whoever holds the keys.

Should I choose primer for a vacant flat?

Primer is recommended on new plaster, patchy or stained walls and big colour changes. Sound existing paint in a similar colour may not need it. We advise after seeing the walls.

Can you also paint doors, grills and cabinets?

Yes. Door, grill, cabinet and wood polishing work can be added to the quotation. See the wood polishing and interior painting pages for details.

Does the 1-year warranty apply?

Yes, for paint peeling, patches and colour fade on surfaces we painted. New cracks and new leakage that was not there before are not covered. Terms are written in your estimate.
