Before we paint: a checklist for Rajajinagar

  • Share the festival or event date as early as possible.
  • Check the terrace and ceilings for stains.
  • Choose colours for the key rooms first.
  • Select which woodwork should be polished.
  • Confirm access for ladders and materials.

Access, society rules and scheduling

We plan the job room by room so the family can keep using the rest of the house, and clean daily. For apartments, we follow association timings and protect common areas.
