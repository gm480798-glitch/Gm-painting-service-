Buildings with several floors need a sequence

Vijayanagar is an established west Bengaluru layout with multi-storey independent buildings, often with rented floors, alongside apartments. Painting such a building needs a sensible order: repairs first, then the exterior and staircase, then each floor in turn.

GM Painting Service plans work floor by floor. Our professional painters coordinate with tenants, protect common stairs and keep the same shade scheme throughout the building so it looks consistent. The result is a neat, uniform finish without the building being disrupted all at once.

What homes in Vijayanagar usually need painted

Staircases and common areas

Staircases take the most scuffs. We protect steps while working and repaint the walls and railings so the building entrance looks well kept.

Rented floors with different tenants

Each floor has its own schedule. We agree dates with each tenant, keep the work contained and avoid blocking shared passages.

Exterior walls on all sides

A tall building has a lot of exterior wall. We inspect each side, repair cracks and plan access before applying primer and exterior coats.
