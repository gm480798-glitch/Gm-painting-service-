A developing area with two kinds of homes

Begur, along Begur Road, is a developing area where new apartment projects are rising next to older homes in the village core. That means two very different painting needs: freshly built flats that want a first proper coat, and older houses with long-weathered walls that need repair before repainting.

Our professional painters adapt to each. In new flats, we focus on putty, primer and clean, even coats. In older homes, we scrape, repair and prime, then recoat for a smooth and neat finish. GM Painting Service offers both fresh painting and repainting in Begur, with a written quotation that explains what is included.

What homes in Begur usually need painted

New flats needing a first proper coat

Builder paint is often a quick coat. We fill small defects, sand and prime, then apply finish coats that look noticeably better and are easier to maintain.

Older homes with weathered exterior walls

Walls in older houses may be chalky or cracked. We clean, repair and prime them before applying exterior coats, so the new paint lasts through the monsoon.

Possession-time touch-ups

On handover day you often find marks and uneven corners. We touch up and recoat those areas so the flat looks finished before you move in.

Begur painting FAQs

Do I paint new flats with primer?

Usually yes. Primer helps the finish coats look even and last. We confirm the system in your written quotation.

Can you repaint an older village-core house?

Yes. We inspect walls, repair cracks, prime and recoat for a durable result.

Do you help at possession time?

Yes. We touch up marks and recoat patchy areas before you move in.

Are the rates the same for new and old homes?

No. Fresh painting needs more preparation, so rates differ. Our cost guide shows the difference.
