Old and new homes side by side

KR Puram, on the Old Madras Road side, mixes older independent houses with newer apartment buildings. The two need different handling: older houses often call for exterior repair and damp checks, while new apartments mainly want a clean, even interior finish.

GM Painting Service covers both with interior and exterior painting. For older houses, our painting team scrapes, repairs and primes before recoating; for apartments, we prepare, mask and apply smooth coats. A written quotation sets out the scope in each case so you know exactly what is included.

What we check before painting in KR Puram

  • Photograph all exterior sides and any stained walls.
  • Check the terrace for water collection.
  • Choose colours for exterior and interior together.
  • Confirm apartment association rules on timings.
  • Decide whether grills and gates are included.

Access, society rules and scheduling

For independent houses we plan ladder or scaffold access and protect entrances and plants. Apartments follow association hours, and we protect lifts and common areas.
