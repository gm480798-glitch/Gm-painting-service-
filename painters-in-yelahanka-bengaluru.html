Larger plots mean larger exteriors

Yelahanka in north Bengaluru has older and newer parts, with independent houses and villas on larger plots, plus newer apartments. Bigger houses carry far more exterior area: front walls, long compound walls, gates and grills that all need protection from sun and rain.

GM Painting Service paints exteriors in a clear sequence: clean, repair, prime and recoat, then treat and repaint metal gates and grills. Our experienced painting team can paint the interiors in the same schedule so the whole property looks coordinated and you deal with one contractor.

Painting jobs we commonly handle in Yelahanka

Long compound walls and rusting gates

Compound walls fade and metal gates rust first. We clean walls, remove rust, apply primer and repaint so the property edge looks sharp again.

Two-storey exteriors needing safe access

Upper walls need proper access. We plan ladders or scaffolding during inspection and cover driveways, plants and vehicles below.

Interior refresh alongside the exterior

Combining jobs saves repeat visits, keeps colours coordinated and lets us sequence work so exterior dust never lands on fresh interior paint.

Before we paint: a checklist for Yelahanka

  • Photograph each exterior side, the compound wall and the gate.
  • Mention rust on grills, gates and railings.
  • Choose exterior colours with the boundary wall in mind.
  • Decide the interior scope if you want one combined schedule.
  • Look over the terrace for cracks and places where water collects.

Access, society rules and scheduling

We plan access to upper walls early and protect the garden, driveway and vehicles before starting. At the end we clean the compound so the house is ready to use again.

Questions about painting in Yelahanka

Do you paint gates and grills?

Yes. We remove rust, apply primer and repaint metal so the finish lasts longer and looks neat.

Can you paint a two-storey house exterior?

Yes. Access for upper walls is planned during inspection so every surface is reached safely.

Can interior and exterior be done together?

Yes, in one schedule. We order the work so exterior dust does not fall on fresh interior paint.

Will my garden be protected?

We cover entrances and vehicles and ask that plants near the walls are moved or wrapped before work starts.
