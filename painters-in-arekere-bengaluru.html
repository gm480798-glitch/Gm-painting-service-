Refreshing a home before you settle in

Arekere, on the Bannerghatta Road side, has a mix of apartment complexes and independent houses or duplexes. Many buyers and tenants repaint before moving in, because the walls they inherit rarely match their taste or condition. Doing it first, before furniture arrives, is the easiest way to get a flawless result.

Our experienced painting team offers fast, neat repainting for Arekere homes. We prepare the walls, fill marks, prime where required and finish with smooth coats, then clean thoroughly so you can move in right away. Balconies and duplex staircases get proper attention, as these areas see more sun and wear.

What homes in Arekere usually need painted

Empty flat repainted before move-in

An empty flat is the ideal time to paint. We can work quickly without furniture constraints and leave the home clean and ready for your belongings.

Balcony walls exposed to sun and rain

Balcony walls fade and peel sooner than interior walls. We scrape, prime and recoat with suitable paint so they hold up better.

Duplex staircases and double-height walls

Higher walls need safe access. We plan scaffolding or ladders for staircases and double-height spaces so coverage is even.

Preparation we plan for Arekere homes

  • Schedule painting before furniture and appliances arrive.
  • Confirm the possession or handover date with the builder or owner.
  • Share the home size and any high walls or staircase heights.
  • Pick colours using samples under your home’s own light.
  • Ask the association about work timings and material movement.

Access, society rules and scheduling

Apartment complexes usually require prior permission, specific working hours and lift protection for material movement. We follow these rules, and for duplexes and independent houses we plan safe access to staircases and upper walls.
