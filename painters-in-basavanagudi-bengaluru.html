Respecting older construction

Basavanagudi is one of the older parts of Bengaluru, and many of its homes have traditional layouts, thick walls and timber doors and windows. Paint on such houses has often been renewed many times, and some walls have never been properly prepared. A good repaint here starts with gentle but thorough surface work.

Our painting team approaches each Basavanagudi house individually. We test plaster, scrape back loose finish, repair small cracks and apply primer so that the new coat is smooth and lasting. We also take care with carved or old woodwork, using polish that shows off the grain. The aim is a neat and clean result that respects the character of the house.

Painting jobs we commonly handle in Basavanagudi

Old plaster that crumbles or powders

Powdery walls will not hold new paint. We brush back loose material, apply a suitable primer and fill dents so the finish coats look even.

Traditional timber doors and windows

Original wood deserves proper care. We sand lightly, remove worn finish and polish in a tone that suits the house, or paint where you prefer.

Narrow lanes and tight access

Delivering materials and working near compound walls can be tricky. We plan loads, ladders and protection for neighbours’ property so the work stays tidy.

Before we paint: a checklist for Basavanagudi

  • Show us any areas where paint peels or plaster crumbles.
  • Tell us which woodwork should be preserved and polished, not painted.
  • Mention the lane width and parking so we can plan materials.
  • Decide whether to keep a traditional colour or try a lighter scheme.
  • Check the terrace for cracks and water collection.

Access, society rules and scheduling

Some Basavanagudi lanes are narrow and parking is limited, so we bring materials in smaller loads and keep the street and neighbouring walls clean. We also take extra care to cover old floors and furniture because they can be harder to replace.

Questions about painting in Basavanagudi

Can old powdery walls be repainted?

Yes, after loose material is brushed off and a primer is applied. Skipping this step can cause the new paint to peel.

Will you paint over my wooden windows?

Only if you want them painted. Many owners prefer polishing to keep the natural wood grain.

How do you work in narrow lanes?

We bring materials in small loads, protect neighbouring walls and plan ladder positions carefully.

Do you repair small cracks first?

Yes. Hairline cracks are filled during preparation; larger structural cracks need separate assessment.
