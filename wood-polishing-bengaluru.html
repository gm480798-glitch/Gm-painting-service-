Wood polishing that brings out the grain

Bengaluru’s sun and monsoon dull wooden doors and furniture over time: finishes crack, peel and lose depth. Polishing renews the finish and protects the wood. We sand away the worn layer, repair small dents, and apply an agreed finish in the sheen and tone you prefer.

Wardrobes and cabinets

Finish depends on the material and existing coating.

Old finish removal scoped after inspection.

Share photos, approximate dimensions, number of pieces and whether the old finish is peeling. Wood polishing is quoted after inspection, because material and the condition of the old finish change the work involved. Combine with interior painting to finish the whole room in one visit.

Frequently asked questions

How long does wood polishing last?

It depends on the wood, the finish and exposure to sun and moisture. Doors facing the sun need more care; we advise on a suitable finish.

Can you change the colour of the wood?

Yes, within limits. We discuss tone and sheen and can show options before work begins.

Will polishing make a mess?

Sanding creates dust. We mask surrounding areas and clean up after the job.

Do you polish old doors with peeling finish?

Yes. The old finish is removed first, then the wood is sanded and polished.

Is wood polishing included in painting packages?

No. It is quoted separately after inspection.
