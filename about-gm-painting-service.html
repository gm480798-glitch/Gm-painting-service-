Who we are

GM Painting Service is a Bengaluru house painting company. We handle interior and exterior painting, rental handover painting, wood polishing, designer textures, terrace and wall waterproofing, tile grouting, roof coating and swimming pool coating from one point of contact. Our experienced painting team is built around neat workmanship and honest quotations.

How we work

Clear scope

Surfaces, preparation, paint quality, coats and price are agreed before we start.

Neat preparation

Furniture covered, floors and fittings masked, and the area cleaned after the job.

Suitable materials

We choose a paint or coating system that suits the surface, with branded options such as Asian Paints, Berger, Dulux and Nerolac.

Written terms

Your estimate lists the scope, exclusions and the warranty terms.

Our 1-year warranty, explained

Covered for 1 year: paint peeling or patches, and colour fade, on the surfaces GM Painting Service painted. We come back and repair them.

Not covered: new cracks that appear in the building, seepage or leakage that appears where there was none before, and water leakage from plumbing, terrace or structure.

We would rather tell you this clearly now than surprise you later.
