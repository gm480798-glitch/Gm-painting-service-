Fresh painting or repainting?

Fresh painting is for new or unpainted walls: they need more preparation, primer and coats. Repainting refreshes already-painted walls. If the old paint is sound it can be done without primer; if it is chalky, patchy or changing colour drastically, repainting with primer gives a better base. Frequently asked questions

How long does interior painting take for a 2 BHK?

It depends on the condition of the walls, the number of coats and whether the home is furnished. We confirm the schedule in the quotation after seeing the site or photos.

Do I need to remove furniture?

No. We cover and move furniture within the home. Clearing fragile items and valuables beforehand helps the work go faster.

Should I choose repainting with primer or without primer?

If existing paint is in good condition, repainting without primer is usually enough. For chalky, stained or patchy walls, primer gives a more even, longer-lasting finish.

What if my walls have damp patches?

Damp patches should be diagnosed first. Painting over active seepage will not last, so we recommend waterproofing or repair before repainting. See our waterproofing page.

Which paint brands do you use?

We work with suitable branded paint systems such as Asian Paints, Berger, Dulux and Nerolac, chosen with you based on budget and surface.
