Rental painting made simple for owners and tenants

Between tenants, walls collect scuffs, nail holes, stains and fading. A fresh coat gets the flat ready to rent faster and helps settle deposit discussions. Our rental packages give you a clear price by home size and paint quality, so you do not have to measure walls or negotiate by the square foot.

Choose unfurnished or furnished (we protect and move furniture), then pick Tractor Emulsion for an affordable refresh, Apcolite Premium for a better finish, or Royale Luxury for a premium look.

Fast turnaround between tenants

Tell us your move-in or handover date when you message us. We plan the schedule around it so your flat is not sitting empty longer than needed. For rented flats with damp patches, we flag them early so they can be fixed before painting.

Frequently asked questions

Are rental packages the final price?

They are starting package prices by home size and paint quality. Heavy repairs, damp treatment or extra items are confirmed in the written quotation.

Do the packages include furniture shifting?

Furnished packages cover protecting and moving furniture within the home. Unfurnished packages assume the flat is empty.

Can I paint only some rooms?

Yes. See the room-wise starting prices on our interior painting page or ask for a custom quote.

Which paint quality should I pick for a rental?

Tractor Emulsion is the economical choice for tenant changes. Premium or Royale Luxury suits higher-rent homes where finish matters more.

Do you paint rental flats in gated communities?

Yes. Please share any association timings or lift rules so we can plan around them.
