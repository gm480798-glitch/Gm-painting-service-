Fresh grout, fresh look

Dirty, cracked or missing grout makes even good tiles look old and lets water seep behind them. Re-grouting restores clean lines and helps keep moisture out. Packages start at ₹2,399; the final scope depends on the area, grout condition and access.

What we check

  • Floor or wall tiles and approximate area.
  • Grout colour, stains, cracks or missing sections.
  • Whether old grout needs removing or only local repair.
  • Curing time and when the area can be used again.

Re-grouting will not fix substrate movement or an underlying leak; those need separate inspection. See waterproofing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does tile grouting cost?

Packages start at ₹2,399. The final price depends on area, grout condition and access.

How long before I can use the area?

Grout needs curing time, which we confirm for the product used.

Can you change the grout colour?

Yes, we can discuss colour options during the quote.

Will re-grouting stop my bathroom leak?

Not by itself if the leak comes from plumbing or the substrate. We inspect and advise.

Do you do kitchen and bathroom tiles?

Yes, walls and floors in kitchens, bathrooms and balconies.
