Before we paint: a checklist for Whitefield

  • Ask your community for contractor entry and approval steps.
  • Share photos of all exterior sides of villas.
  • Confirm lift and material movement rules.
  • Choose colours for all rooms before we begin.
  • Provide a contact for weekday access if you are away.

Access, society rules and scheduling

Gate entry, working hours and material movement are agreed with your community. We protect lifts and corridors, stay within noise windows, and keep the work area clean so neighbours are not troubled.
