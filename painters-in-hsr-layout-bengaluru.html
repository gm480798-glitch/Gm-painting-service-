HSR Layout painting FAQs

Do you paint the exterior of independent houses in HSR Layout?

Yes. We repair cracks, treat peeling paint and apply primer and exterior coats, with access arranged according to the building height.

Can the family stay at home during painting?

Usually yes. We work room by room and cover furniture, though strong paint smells are easier to manage with open windows.

Should I choose primer or no primer for repainting?

If the existing paint is sound, repainting without primer is often enough. Chalky or patchy walls benefit from primer for an even look.

Can you also fix damp walls before repainting?

We inspect and advise. Seepage must be treated at its source first, and our waterproofing service can handle terrace and wall treatment.
