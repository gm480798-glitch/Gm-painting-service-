Stop seepage before it ruins your paint

Damp patches, peeling paint and musty walls usually mean water is getting in somewhere: a terrace, an exterior wall crack, a bathroom, or a pipe. Repainting over it only hides the problem for a few months. We inspect the likely source, recommend a treatment, and then repaint so the wall looks good again.

Frequently asked questions

Can you waterproof my terrace?

Yes. We inspect the terrace, treat cracks and joints, and apply a suitable waterproofing coating. The rate is indicative per sq ft and confirmed at inspection.

Why is my wall damp?

Common causes are terrace seepage, cracks in exterior walls, bathroom or pipe leaks, and rising damp. We help identify the likely source.

Can I repaint damp walls right away?

Not until the cause is treated and the wall dries, otherwise paint will blister and peel again.

How long does waterproofing take to cure?

Curing time depends on the system. We confirm it in writing so you know when the terrace can be used.

Do you offer reflective roof coating?

Yes, protective and reflective roof coating options such as LuminX are available after checking the roof.
