Older homes with character deserve patient work

Malleshwaram is one of the older parts of Bengaluru, with many traditional houses, timber doors and windows, and compact rooms. Good painting here respects the house: thorough preparation, polished woodwork, tidy edges and no rush.

GM Painting Service takes this approach. Our painting team checks old plaster, repairs cracks, primes surfaces and paints neatly while polishing doors and windows to a warm, even finish. We aim for excellent finishing that suits the character of the home rather than hiding it.

Common painting requests in Malleshwaram

Old plaster with cracks and powdery patches

Older plaster can crumble or chalk. We brush away loose material, repair cracks and prime so the new paint grips properly.

Timber doors and windows that show their age

Original wood looks better polished, not painted over. We sand lightly, remove worn finish and polish in a tone that suits the home.

Compact rooms with tight edges

In small rooms, edges and corners show every mistake. Careful masking and controlled application keep lines crisp.

What we check before painting in Malleshwaram

  • Mark cracks, crumbling plaster and flaking areas for us.
  • Decide which woodwork to polish and which to paint.
  • Tell us about lane width and parking for materials.
  • Choose between traditional colours and a lighter scheme.
  • Check the terrace for cracks and standing water.

Access, society rules and scheduling

Malleshwaram lanes can be narrow and parking limited, so we bring materials in smaller loads and protect neighbouring walls. Old floors and furniture are covered carefully because they can be hard to replace.
