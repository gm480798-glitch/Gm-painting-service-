What we check before painting in Bommanahalli

  • Share the move-out and next move-in dates.
  • Photograph stains, nail holes and damp spots.
  • Pick a neutral shade for easier future touch-ups.
  • Tell us if furniture is still inside the flat.
  • Check the association’s working-hour rules.

Access, society rules and scheduling

Bommanahalli apartments often limit working hours and lift usage, which we follow. In compact independent buildings, we protect stairs and passages and keep the working area tidy so neighbours are not disturbed.
