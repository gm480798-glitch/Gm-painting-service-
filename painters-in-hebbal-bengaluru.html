Frequently asked questions from Hebbal

What is a washable paint?

A good-quality emulsion that can be lightly wiped without the colour coming off. It suits dusty homes, children’s rooms and kitchens.

Do you check for damp before repainting?

Yes. Damp patches on lower walls are inspected first, because painting over active moisture will blister and peel again.

Do you paint independent houses as well?

Yes, both interiors and exteriors, with crack repair and primer where needed.

Can you work within apartment rules?

Yes. We follow your association’s hours, protect lifts and corridors and clean up daily.
