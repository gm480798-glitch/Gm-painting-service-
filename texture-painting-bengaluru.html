Planning your texture wall

  • Pick the wall: choose the one your eye goes to first.
  • Ask for a sample on a small patch when practical, as colour changes with light.
  • Keep the other walls simple so the feature wall stands out.

Texture rates depend on pattern, product, preparation and measured area, so we quote after seeing the wall or photos. Add a plain interior repaint for the rest of the room.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a texture wall cost?

It depends on the pattern, product and area. We quote after viewing the wall; send a photo on WhatsApp.

Can I see a sample first?

Where practical we show samples. Colour and texture can vary with lighting and wall condition.

Does the wall need preparation?

Yes. Cracks and dents are repaired and the surface is primed so the texture adheres evenly.

Can textures be done in rental homes?

Yes, but plain repainting is more common for tenant handovers. Check with the owner.

How do I clean a textured wall?

Dust gently with a soft brush; avoid scrubbing. We advise on care for the specific finish.
