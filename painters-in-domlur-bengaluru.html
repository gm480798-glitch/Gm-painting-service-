Small spaces, high standards

Domlur sits next to Indiranagar and has a mix of older independent houses and compact apartment buildings, many of them rented. Rooms here tend to be modest in size, and neat work stands out: crisp corners, clean skirting lines and even coats in tight spaces.

GM Painting Service brings professional painters who are comfortable in compact homes. We mask carefully, work around furniture and keep dust and drips under control. For older houses we also offer wood polishing for doors and frames so the paint and woodwork look cohesive.

Domlur painting FAQs

Can you paint a small flat neatly?

Yes. We mask edges, cover furniture and keep drips and dust under control.

Do you polish wooden doors too?

Yes. We sand and polish doors and frames to match the refreshed walls.

Which colours suit small rooms?

Light, warm neutrals help rooms feel larger. We can advise on sheen as well.

Can you work around my furniture?

Yes. We cover items and paint in sections when full removal is not possible.
