Fresh painting or repainting — how to decide

  • Fresh painting: new construction, bare plaster or putty, or walls stripped back to the surface.
  • Repainting without primer: sound existing paint, a similar colour and a quick refresh.
  • Repainting with primer: older, chalky or patchy paint, stains, or a big colour change.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between fresh painting and repainting?

Fresh painting is for new or unpainted surfaces and needs more preparation and coats. Repainting refreshes walls that are already painted.

When do I need primer?

Primer helps on chalky, patchy or stained walls, new putty, and large colour changes. Sound existing paint may not need it.

Are these the final rates?

They are indicative per sq ft rates. The final quote depends on the measured area, repairs, putty and paint quality.

How do I know my paintable area?

You do not need to measure. Share your home size and photos and we will estimate; the final area is confirmed on site.

Which paint quality should I choose?

Tractor Emulsion is the affordable option, Premium gives a better finish and durability, and Royal Luxury is the top range for a rich look.
