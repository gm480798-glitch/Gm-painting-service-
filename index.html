What is included in GM Painting Service packages?

We confirm the exact paint brand, product range and quantity with you before work starts. We will not silently substitute a cheaper paint.

01 · Surface preparationBasic scraping of loose paint, sanding and minor patch/crack filling as agreed after inspection.
02 · Primer & puttyPrimer and putty are included only to the extent listed in the final written quotation. Full-wall putty or major plaster repair may cost extra.
03 · Paint coatsStandard package proposal: 1 primer coat where required and 2 finish coats. Exact system depends on the wall condition and selected paint.
04 · Paint brand choiceAsian Paints, Berger or Dulux can be quoted in the selected range. The specific product name and finish (economy, premium, washable, etc.) will be written in the quote before booking.
05 · Protection & clean-upBasic masking/protection of nearby fixtures and a work-area clean-up are included when stated in the confirmed scope.
06 · Not included by defaultMajor seepage treatment, active leakage, extensive putty/plaster repair, texture, doors, grills, cabinets, balcony/exterior and furniture shifting are separate unless explicitly listed.

Final material, product, preparation, included rooms and exclusions are confirmed in writing before work begins.
