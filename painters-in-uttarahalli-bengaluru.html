Getting a newly built house painted properly

Uttarahalli is a growing area on the Kanakapura Road side, where many independent houses are built on individual sites and new apartments continue to appear. A newly built house arrives with bare plaster, and the quality of the first paint job decides how the walls look for a long time.

GM Painting Service specialises in careful fresh painting for homes like these. Our professional painters check the plaster, apply putty where it improves the finish, prime the surface and then give the agreed number of finish coats. We also handle touch-ups on builder-finish walls, so a handed-over apartment can look properly finished.

Painting jobs we commonly handle in Uttarahalli

Bare plaster in a newly built house

New plaster should be dry and cured before painting. We check the surface, fill small defects, sand and prime, then apply finish coats for an even look.

Builder-finish apartments needing touch-ups

Handed-over flats often have patchy paint, marks from other trades and uneven corners. We repair and recoat to bring the whole flat to a consistent finish.

Exterior and compound wall for a new house

A new house usually needs the exterior painted as well. We apply primer and exterior coats to the front, sides and compound wall.

Before we paint: a checklist for Uttarahalli

  • Make sure plaster is fully dry and cured before painting.
  • Decide the colour scheme and finish for each room in advance.
  • Tell us if electrical and plumbing work is complete.
  • Share the floor plan or home size for an accurate quote.
  • Ask for putty and primer details in the quotation.

Access, society rules and scheduling

New construction sites can have dust, open fittings and wet areas, so we plan painting after major civil and electrical work is finished. We protect floors, windows, fittings and tiles, and coordinate with your builder or contractor if others are still working.

Questions about painting in Uttarahalli

How long should I wait after plastering before painting?

Plaster must be dry and cured. We advise based on the surface; painting too early can cause patches and peeling.

Do you apply putty and primer?

Yes, where needed. We confirm the putty and primer details in your written quotation.

Can you touch up my apartment after handover?

Yes. We repair marks and recoat patchy areas to give a consistent finish.

Do you also paint the compound wall?

Yes, with exterior primer and coats as part of the exterior scope.
