Tall buildings, strict routines

Bellandur, along the Outer Ring Road, has large high-rise communities with many tenants. Painting in a tower is different from painting a house: materials travel by service lift, work hours are fixed, and neighbours are very close. A neat, predictable process matters more than anything.

GM Painting Service plans Bellandur jobs around these constraints. Our professional painters protect lifts and corridors, carry materials in the permitted windows and paint room by room. We handle tenant-change repaints, owner refreshes and balcony walls with the same tidy approach and a smooth finish.

Common painting requests in Bellandur

Service-lift windows and corridor protection

Balcony walls in tall towers

Flats change occupants often. We offer fixed packages and a quick schedule so the flat is ready for the next tenant.

In Bellandur towers we use protective covering for lifts and keep materials inside the flat rather than in corridors. We stay within allowed hours, minimise noise and clean thoroughly each day so neighbours are not affected.
