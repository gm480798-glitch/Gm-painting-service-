Painting for a rental-driven apartment market

Electronic City, along the Hosur Road corridor, is a tech-workforce area with many apartments and gated communities where owners often live elsewhere and let their flats. Long tenancies leave scuffs, fingerprints and faded patches that deserve a proper repaint before the next tenant.

GM Painting Service handles these handover repaints efficiently. Our professional painters repair marks, clean greasy areas, apply primer where needed and finish with smooth coats, so the flat looks fresh in photographs and on viewing day. We also coordinate with your gated community’s rules from the first message.

Painting jobs we commonly handle in Electronic City

End-of-tenancy wall scuffs

After a long tenancy, walls near doors, beds and kitchens show scuffs. We wash, fill and recoat so the whole flat looks even again.

Owners managing from far away

Many owners are not on site. We share photos and written quotations, and coordinate with a caretaker or agent for access and handover.

Gated-community timing rules

Large communities set working hours and entry procedures. We follow them to avoid delays or objections from neighbours.

Before we paint: a checklist for Electronic City

  • Share a contact person who can give access if you are away.
  • Photograph the worst scuffs, stains and damp spots.
  • Check your community’s contractor and entry process.
  • Choose a neutral shade that suits future tenants.
  • Confirm handover and viewing dates.

Access, society rules and scheduling

We coordinate gate entry, work timings and lift protection with your community, and keep photographic updates for owners who are away. At the end of the job, the flat is cleaned and handed to your caretaker or tenant as agreed.

Questions about painting in Electronic City

I live in another city. Can you paint my flat?

Yes. Share photos, agree the quote on WhatsApp and nominate someone for access; we send progress photos.

Do you work in gated communities?

Yes, following their entry and timing rules.

How do you handle kitchen grease on walls?

We clean and prepare greasy areas before painting, because paint will not stick well to oily surfaces.

Which paint quality suits a rented flat?

Tractor Emulsion is the affordable option, while Premium suits higher-rent homes.
