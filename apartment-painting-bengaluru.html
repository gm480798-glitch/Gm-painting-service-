What is different about painting an apartment

Most apartment communities in Bengaluru have rules on contractor entry, working hours, lift use and material shifting. Walls are often shared with neighbours, balconies are small but exposed, and ceilings can be affected by leaks from the flat above. Our professional painters plan for all of this before work begins, so the job is smooth for you and for the neighbours.

Society permission

Ask your association what it needs, such as entry passes, work timings and lift booking, and share it with us before the start date.

Neat masking

Floors, switchboards, windows and fittings are covered, and the flat is cleaned before handover.

Balcony and utility walls

Exposed walls need the right primer and coat, and are planned separately from the living areas.

Ceiling and damp checks

Stains are inspected first, because new paint over an active leak will fail.

Before the painters arrive: a short checklist

  • Confirm contractor entry rules, working hours and lift or service-lift access with your association.
  • Decide your colours and paint quality, or ask us to suggest options for each room.
  • Clear small valuables and fragile items. We cover and protect the furniture that stays.
  • Share photos of every wall, the ceiling and the balcony so the quotation is accurate.
  • Tell us about any damp patch or past leakage, even if it is dry now.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my society's permission to paint inside my flat?

Many communities ask contractors to register, follow working hours and use a designated lift. Check with your association and tell us the rules, and we will plan the schedule around them.

Can you paint just one room or a few walls?

Yes. You can choose one wall, a few walls, a bedroom or the full flat. See the interior painting page for the options.

There is a stain on my ceiling. Will painting fix it?

Not by itself. If water is coming from above, the source must be fixed first. We inspect and advise, and waterproofing can be quoted separately if needed.

Is repainting with primer better than without primer?

Primer helps on chalky, patchy or stained walls and big colour changes. For sound walls in a similar colour, repainting without primer can be enough and is more affordable.

What does the 1-year warranty cover in an apartment?

Paint peeling, patches and colour fade on surfaces we painted. It does not cover new cracks or new seepage that was not there before. Terms are in your estimate.
