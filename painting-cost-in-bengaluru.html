What changes the painting cost?

  • Total wall and ceiling area.
  • Paint quality: Tractor, Premium or Royal Luxury.
  • Fresh painting or repainting, and whether primer and putty are needed.
  • Cracks, dampness or seepage that need repair first.
  • Furnished or unfurnished home.

Frequently asked questions

What are the painting charges in Bengaluru?

Repainting starts from ₹7 per sq ft and fresh painting from ₹16 per sq ft (Tractor Emulsion) with GM Painting Service. Premium and Royal options cost more, and rental-flat packages start at ₹8,999 for an unfurnished 1 BHK. Final price is confirmed after inspection.

How much does it cost to paint 1,000 sq ft?

At our indicative rates, 1,000 sq ft of paintable area costs about ₹7,000 to ₹19,000 for repainting without primer, ₹9,000 to ₹22,000 for repainting with primer, and ₹16,000 to ₹33,000 for fresh painting, depending on paint quality. Measured area, putty and repairs decide the final quote.

What is the painting cost for 1 BHK in Bengaluru?

Rental-flat packages for an unfurnished 1 BHK start at ₹8,999 with Tractor Emulsion, ₹11,999 with Apcolite Premium and ₹14,999 with Royale Luxury. Furnished homes cost ₹1,000 more.

What is the painting rate per sq ft in Bengaluru?

Repainting without primer is ₹7, ₹14 and ₹19 per sq ft for Tractor, Premium and Royal Luxury emulsion. Repainting with primer is ₹9, ₹17 and ₹22. Fresh painting is ₹16, ₹19 and ₹33. These are indicative rates.

What is the painting cost for 2 BHK and 3 BHK?

Unfurnished 2 BHK packages start at ₹13,999 and 3 BHK at ₹17,999 (Tractor Emulsion). Premium and Royale options and furnished prices are in the table above.

Is the price final?

No. These are starting prices. Wall condition, repairs, dampness, primer, putty, ceilings and furniture movement can change the scope, so we confirm everything in a written estimate before work starts.
