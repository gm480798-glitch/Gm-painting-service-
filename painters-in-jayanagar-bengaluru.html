Older houses need preparation before paint

Jayanagar is a planned residential layout laid out in numbered blocks, and many of its houses are older independent homes with layers of paint built up over time. Painting such walls well means spending more effort on preparation than on the colour itself: scraping flaking areas, repairing plaster and sealing before the finish coats.

GM Painting Service brings a patient approach to Jayanagar homes. Our professional painters inspect old plaster, check for damp, and recommend primer where it will improve adhesion. The result is excellent finishing on walls that may be many decades old, along with polish work for the doors and window frames that give these houses their character.

What homes in Jayanagar usually need painted

Layers of old paint and flaking patches

Old distemper or heavily recoated walls can flake when new paint is applied over them. We scrape back unsound areas, fill and sand, then prime so the new coat bonds well.

Seepage marks on older walls

Older plaster can hold moisture, showing as patches near the base or under windows. We find the likely source and recommend treatment before repainting.

Wooden doors and windows needing polish

Jayanagar homes often keep original timber doors and window frames. Fresh polish brings the grain back and makes repainted walls look even better.

Jayanagar painting FAQs

Do you scrape off old flaking paint first?

Where paint is flaking or unsound, yes. Good preparation is what makes the new coat smooth and long-lasting.

Should I use primer on old walls?

Often yes. Primer helps new paint stick to chalky or patched surfaces and gives a more even colour.

Can you polish my original wooden doors?

Yes. We sand the worn finish, repair small dents and polish to your preferred tone and sheen.

What if the damp keeps coming back?

Painting alone will not stop it. We inspect for the source and suggest waterproofing before repainting.
