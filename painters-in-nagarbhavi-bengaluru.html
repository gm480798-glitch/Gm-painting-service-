Exterior and boundary walls face full weather. We repair cracks, prime and recoat for a lasting finish.

What we check before painting in Nagarbhavi

  • Confirm plaster is fully dry on new houses.
  • Check the terrace for cracks and pooling water.
  • Choose exterior and interior colours together.
  • Share photos of all exterior sides.
  • Decide whether gates and grills are included.

Access, society rules and scheduling

We plan access to exterior walls and the terrace early, protect the entrance, plants and vehicles and clean the property at the end of the job.
