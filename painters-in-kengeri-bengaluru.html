Spacious new homes need the right sequence of coats

Kengeri, on the Mysuru Road side, has independent houses on larger sites and newer apartments, many of them recently built. Fresh painting on new construction has to follow a proper order of putty, primer and finish coats, or the walls can look patchy within months.

GM Painting Service delivers that sequence and also paints compound walls and exteriors. Our experienced painting team coordinates with your builder or contractor, finishes room by room and cleans up after itself, so the home is ready to move into.
