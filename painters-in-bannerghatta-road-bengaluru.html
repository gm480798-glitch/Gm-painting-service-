What we check before painting in Bannerghatta Road

  • Ask the society management about contractor registration and gate rules.
  • Confirm the permitted working hours and lift usage.
  • Share home size and number of rooms for an accurate plan.
  • Plan where materials will be stored inside the home.
  • Decide colours for all rooms before the first day.

Access, society rules and scheduling

In gated communities, material is carried in during permitted hours and lifts are protected as required. We keep noise within allowed timings, clean up at the end of each day and avoid blocking corridors shared with neighbours.
