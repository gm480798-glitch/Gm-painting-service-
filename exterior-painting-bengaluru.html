Exterior painting that protects as well as it looks

Your home’s exterior faces Bengaluru’s sun and monsoon every year. A good exterior paint job is mostly preparation: removing loose paint, repairing cracks, treating algae and damp patches, and applying the right primer before the finish coats. Our professional painters plan every exterior job around access, height and weather so the finish looks excellent and holds up.

We paint independent houses, villas, compound walls, balconies, parapets, gates and grills, and we can combine exterior and interior painting in one schedule.

What decides the cost of exterior painting

  • Height and access: scaffolding or ladders for upper floors.
  • Surface condition: cracks, peeling paint and algae need repair first.
  • Paint system: standard exterior emulsion or a premium weather-protection range.
  • Extras: compound walls, grills, gates, balconies and waterproofing repairs.

Good to know

If a wall has seepage, painting alone will not solve it. We recommend fixing the water source first; see waterproofing in Bengaluru. Our warranty covers paint peeling, patches and colour fade on the surfaces we paint, and does not cover new cracks or new seepage in the building.

Frequently asked questions

Is exterior painting possible in the monsoon?

Dry, clear days are best. We plan around the weather and avoid painting damp walls, because moisture trapped under paint leads to peeling.

Do you provide scaffolding?

Access depends on the building height and layout. We confirm how the upper floors will be reached when we inspect the site or review photos.

Can you repair cracks before painting?

Yes, surface cracks are filled and treated as part of preparation. Structural cracks need a separate assessment and are outside our warranty.

Is exterior painting in a gated community allowed?

Most communities require committee permission, and sometimes a colour approval. Check with your association; we can work to their timings.

How do I get a price?

Send photos of each side of the house on WhatsApp and we will discuss scope; the final price is confirmed after inspection.
